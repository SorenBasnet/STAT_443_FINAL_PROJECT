\subsubsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

This report explores patients and genomic dataset of \textasciitilde1000
patients of chronic lymphocytic leukemia and Monoclonal B-cell
Lymphocytosis. Primarily affecting population of European Heritage,
chronic lymphocytic leukemia is a form of luekemia that affects blood
and bone marrow. Monoclonal B-cell Lymphocytosis is a precursor to the
chronic lymphocytic leukemia. This paper used GUIDE to determine
importance score of variables towards survival of patients surviving 5
years after the diagnosis date. This paper also used guide to plot
survival plots and determine factors affecting survival from the data
used.
